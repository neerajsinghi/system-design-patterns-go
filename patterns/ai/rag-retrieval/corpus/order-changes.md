# Changing or Cancelling an Order

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge within 1 hour of placing it, from the order page. After that hour the order is sent to the warehouse and cannot be cancelled; once it arrives you can return it under the returns policy.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed until the order is packed. Once it is packed, we cannot redirect the parcel, but the carrier may let you change the address from the tracking page.

## Adding items

Items cannot be added to an order that has been placed. Place a second order instead; if both orders ship together, the second shipping charge is refunded.
