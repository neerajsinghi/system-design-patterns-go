# Customer Data and Privacy

## What we keep

Order records are kept for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes. Browsing history on the website is kept for 13 months and then deleted.

## Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and personal data to be deleted from the privacy page. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days, except for order records we must keep by law.

## Marketing emails

Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect within 48 hours. Order and delivery emails are not marketing and are still sent.
