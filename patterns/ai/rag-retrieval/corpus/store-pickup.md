# In-Store Pickup

## When your order is ready

Click-and-collect orders are usually ready for pickup within 2 hours during store opening times. We send a text message when the order is ready.

## Collecting the order

Bring photo ID and the order confirmation. Someone else can collect on your behalf if you add their name to the order.

## How long we hold orders

Orders are held for 7 days. Orders not collected after 7 days are cancelled and refunded automatically.
