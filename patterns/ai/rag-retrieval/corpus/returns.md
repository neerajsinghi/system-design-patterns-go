# Returns and Refunds

Pebblebrook Home is a fictional home-goods retailer. This policy covers items bought on pebblebrook.example or in a Pebblebrook store.

## Return window

Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The return window was extended from 14 days to 30 days on 1 March 2026, and the longer window applies to every order delivered on or after that date.

To start a return, open the order in your account and choose Return item. You will get a prepaid label by email.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused and in their original packaging with all parts and manuals included. Furniture that has been assembled can still be returned if it is undamaged, but a 15% restocking fee applies to assembled furniture.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse inspects the item. Inspection usually happens within two days of the parcel arriving. Shipping charges from the original order are not refunded unless the item was faulty.

## Items that cannot be returned

Mattresses that have been unsealed, custom-cut fabric, personalised engraved items and final-sale clearance items cannot be returned. They are still covered by the warranty if they are faulty.
