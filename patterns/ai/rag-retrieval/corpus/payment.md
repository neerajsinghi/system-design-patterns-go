# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and Pebblebrook gift cards. Cryptocurrency and cheques are not accepted.

## Split payments

An order can be paid with at most two payment methods, for example a gift card plus a credit card.

## Paying in instalments

Orders over $150 can be paid in four interest-free instalments through our financing partner Paywise, a fictional lender. The first instalment is taken at checkout and the rest every two weeks.

## When you are charged

Cards are authorised at checkout and charged when the order ships. Large-item deliveries are charged when the delivery is booked.
