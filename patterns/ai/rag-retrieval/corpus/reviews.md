# Product Review Guidelines

## Who can review

Anyone with a verified purchase can review a product, once the order has been delivered. Each customer can post one review per product.

## Moderation

Reviews are checked before publishing, usually within 72 hours. Reviews that contain personal information, links, or offensive language are rejected.

## Incentivised reviews

Reviews written in exchange for a free product or a discount are marked as incentivised. Pebblebrook never pays for positive reviews.
