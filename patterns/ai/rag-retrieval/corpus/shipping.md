# Shipping Options

## Standard shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. It is free on orders over $50; below that it costs a flat $6.95. Orders are packed at the nearest of our three warehouses.

## Express shipping

Express shipping delivers on the next business day if the order is placed before 2 pm local time. It costs $12 per order and is free for Pebble Plus members. Express is not available for furniture that needs a delivery team.

## International shipping

We ship to 14 countries, listed at checkout. International orders take 7 to 12 business days. Import duties and local taxes are paid by the customer when the parcel arrives and are not included in the price at checkout.

## Tracking

Every parcel gets a tracking number by email once it leaves the warehouse. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.
