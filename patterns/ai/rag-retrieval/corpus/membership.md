# Pebble Plus Membership

## Price and benefits

Pebble Plus costs $49 per year. Members get free express shipping, early access to sales and double Pebble Points during the member week each spring.

## Cancelling membership

You can cancel Pebble Plus at any time from your account. Cancelling within the first 30 days gives a full refund if you have not used any member benefit; after that, the membership stays active until the end of the paid year and is not refunded.

## Renewal

Membership renews automatically each year. We email a reminder 7 days before the renewal charge.
