# Haul-Away and Recycling

## Removing old furniture

When we deliver a large item, the delivery team can take away one old item of the same type for $25. Book haul-away at checkout; it cannot be added on the delivery day.

## What happens to it

Hauled-away items are donated if they are in good condition, and otherwise recycled through a certified partner. Mattresses are always recycled, never donated.

## What we cannot take

We cannot take items containing hazardous materials, items infested with pests, or appliances that still contain fuel or refrigerant.
