# Assembly Service

## Booking assembly

Professional assembly can be added to most furniture for $79 per item. It must be booked at least 3 days before the assembly date.

## Where it is available

The assembly service is available in 40 metro areas. Enter your postcode on the product page to check whether your address is covered.

## Cancelling assembly

Assembly can be cancelled for a full refund up to 48 hours before the appointment. Later cancellations are charged half the fee.
