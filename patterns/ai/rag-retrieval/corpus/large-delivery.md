# Large-Item Delivery

Sofas, beds, wardrobes and other large items are delivered by a two-person team instead of a parcel carrier.

## Delivery window

You choose a delivery day at checkout, and the evening before you receive a 4-hour arrival window by text message. The team brings the item into the room of your choice and removes the packaging.

## Rescheduling

You can reschedule for free up to 24 hours before the delivery day. Rescheduling later than that, or not being home when the team arrives, costs a $30 redelivery fee.

## Access requirements

Please measure doors, stairs and lifts before ordering. If the item cannot be brought in safely the team will leave it at the entrance, and a return for that reason is free.
