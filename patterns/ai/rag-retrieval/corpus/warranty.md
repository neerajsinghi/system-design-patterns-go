# Product Warranty

## What the warranty covers

Furniture comes with a 2-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Lamps, small appliances and other electronics have a 1-year warranty. The warranty starts on the delivery date.

## Making a warranty claim

Submit a claim through the support portal with your order number and photos of the defect. Approved claims are repaired, replaced or refunded at our choice, usually within 10 business days.

## Exclusions

The warranty does not cover normal wear, damage from misuse, outdoor use of indoor furniture, or items that were modified. Fabric fading caused by direct sunlight is treated as normal wear.
