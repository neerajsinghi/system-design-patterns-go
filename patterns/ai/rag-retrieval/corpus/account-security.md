# Account Security

## Password resets

A password reset link is sent to the email on the account and is valid for 20 minutes. Support staff will never ask you for your password.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication can be turned on in account settings using an authenticator app. It is required for business accounts.

## Locked accounts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes. If it is locked again on the same day, you must reset the password to unlock it.
