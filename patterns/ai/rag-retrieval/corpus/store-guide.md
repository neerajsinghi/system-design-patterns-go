# Visiting a Pebblebrook Store

This guide answers common questions about visiting one of our showrooms. Online orders, deliveries and returns are covered by their own policies.

## Store opening hours

Showrooms are open from 10 am to 9 pm Monday to Saturday and from 11 am to 6 pm on Sunday. Opening hours on public holidays are posted on each store's page a week in advance. The café inside larger showrooms closes one hour before the store does, and the last design consultation of the day starts two hours before closing.

## Parking

Every showroom has a car park. Parking is free for the first 2 hours if you validate your ticket at any till; after that the car park operator's normal rates apply. Spaces next to the entrance are reserved for blue-badge holders and for customers collecting large orders, who can ask a member of staff to help load their car.

## Pets in the store

Dogs on a short lead are welcome in all showrooms, but they must stay off the furniture on display. Assistance animals are welcome everywhere, including the café. Water bowls are available at the entrance. Other pets should stay at home, because the showroom floor is busy and some customers are allergic.

## Accessibility

All showrooms are step-free, with lifts to every floor. Wheelchairs can be borrowed free of charge at the information desk, and a quiet hour with music off and lights lowered runs every Tuesday from 10 am to 11 am. Staff can carry small items to the tills for you on request.

## Design consultations

A free 45-minute design consultation with one of our interior designers can be booked online or at the information desk. Bring room measurements and photos, and the designer will put together a plan and a shopping list that you can take home or order from straight away.

## Fabric swatches

You can take up to 5 free fabric swatches from the swatch wall in any showroom, or order them online and have them posted to you within 3 days. Swatches do not need to be returned. Large sample pieces of upholstery fabric cost $5 each, refunded if you order a sofa in that fabric within 60 days.

## Floor models

Display pieces that are being replaced are sold as floor models at 20% off. Floor models are sold as seen and count as final-sale clearance items, so they cannot be returned, but they keep the normal warranty. Ask staff about floor models, because they are not listed online.

## Children in the store

Children under 8 must be supervised by an adult at all times. Larger showrooms have a free play area for children aged 3 to 7, open for up to 60 minutes per visit. Baby-changing rooms are next to the toilets on every floor.
