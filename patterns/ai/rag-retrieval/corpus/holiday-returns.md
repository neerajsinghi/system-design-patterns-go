# Holiday Extended Returns

Gifts are often opened weeks after they are bought, so returns are relaxed over the holiday season.

## Extended holiday window

Orders placed between 1 November and 24 December can be returned until 31 January of the following year. If the standard 30-day window ends later than 31 January, the later date applies.

## Gift receipts

A gift receipt lets the recipient return an item without the price being shown. Refunds for gift returns are issued as a Pebblebrook gift card in the recipient's name, not to the buyer's card, so the gift stays a surprise.

## What the holiday extension does not cover

The extension does not apply to items bought with a business account, to Pebble Plus membership fees, or to items that cannot be returned under the standard policy.
