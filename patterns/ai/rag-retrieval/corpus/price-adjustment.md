# Price Adjustments

## Our own price drops

If the price of an item drops within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference as a credit to your original payment method. Ask through chat or the order page, quoting your order number.

## Competitor prices

Pebblebrook Home does not match competitor prices, and adjustments are not given for prices from other retailers or marketplaces.

## Exclusions

Price adjustments are not given for clearance items, flash sales lasting under 24 hours, or prices paid with a promotional code.
