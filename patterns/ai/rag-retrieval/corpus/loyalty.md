# Pebble Points Loyalty Programme

## Earning points

Members earn 1 Pebble Point for every dollar spent, after discounts and before tax. Points appear in your account when the order ships.

## Redeeming points

Every 500 points can be redeemed for a $10 credit at checkout. Credits can be combined with promotional codes but not with other credits on the same order.

## Expiry

Points expire after 18 months without any account activity. A purchase, a return or a review resets the 18-month clock.

## Tiers

Members who spend more than $1,000 in a calendar year reach Gold tier and earn 1.5 points per dollar for the following year.
