# Customer Support

## Opening hours

Customer support is open from 8 am to 8 pm Eastern Time, Monday to Saturday. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Contact channels

Live chat usually answers within 2 minutes during opening hours. Emails are answered within 24 hours, and phone support is available on the same hours as chat.

## Escalations

If you are not happy with an answer, ask for a supervisor. Escalated cases get a reply from a team lead within 2 business days.
