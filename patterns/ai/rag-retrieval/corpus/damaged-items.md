# Damaged or Missing Items

## Reporting damage

If an item arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and the box. Reports made later are handled as warranty claims instead.

## Replacements

For damage reported in time, we ship a replacement at no cost and send a prepaid label for the damaged item. If the item is out of stock, you get a full refund including shipping.

## Missing parcels

If tracking shows a parcel as delivered but you cannot find it, wait one business day and then contact support. We open an investigation with the carrier and resend the order once it is confirmed lost.
