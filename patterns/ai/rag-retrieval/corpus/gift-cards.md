# Gift Cards

## Validity

Pebblebrook gift cards never expire and have no monthly fees. They can be used online and in all stores.

## Cash redemption

Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, except where local law requires a cash refund of a small remaining balance.

## Lost or stolen cards

We can replace a lost or stolen gift card with the remaining balance if you have the original receipt or the order confirmation email. Balances that were already spent before you report the loss cannot be recovered.
