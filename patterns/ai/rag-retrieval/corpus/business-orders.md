# Business and Bulk Orders

## Bulk discount

Orders of 10 or more units of the same item get an 8% discount, applied automatically at checkout.

## Invoicing

Approved business accounts can pay by invoice on net-30 terms. Applying for a business account takes about 3 business days and needs a company registration number.

## Returns for business orders

Business orders can be returned within 14 days of delivery, and a 10% restocking fee applies to bulk returns of 10 or more units.
